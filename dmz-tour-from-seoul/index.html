DMZ Tour from Seoul: Third Tunnel, Dora Observatory and the JSA

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 29, 20266 min read

Quick answer

  • Foreign visitors can't tour the Joint Security Area (JSA/Panmunjeom) at the moment: the Ministry of Unification says it isn't running tours for foreigners or groups. You can still visit the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory on Paju City's DMZ Peace Tour from Imjingak.
  • The tour costs ₩12,200 for adults with the monorail into the tunnel (₩9,200 if you walk down) and takes about 3 hours. Tickets are sold, and reserved tickets collected, at Imjingak 09:00–14:30, and they often sell out early. It's closed on Mondays, weekday public holidays and the main day of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Bring your original passport — no copies, photos or expired passports. Photos and videos are banned inside the tour area. From Nov 23 to 30, 2026, the road to Dora Observatory is closed, so on tour days in that period only the Third Tunnel is open.
Recent changes to know
  • Oct 23, 2026Paju City notice — Dora Observatory road fully closed Nov 23 (Mon) to Nov 30 (Mon), 2026. On those days only the Third Tunnel can be visited and tickets are sold on site only.

You can visit the DMZ from Seoul on your own: Paju City's DMZ Peace Tour leaves from Imjingak, north of Seoul, and goes to the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory in about 3 hours for ₩12,200 (adult, with the tunnel monorail). Bring your original passport, and don't go on a Monday — the tour is closed. The Joint Security Area (JSA, Panmunjeom) isn't open to foreign visitors at the moment.

Source: Paju City DMZ Peace Tour and fees (Korean), and Korea Tourism Organization; checked Oct 28, 2026.

The DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) is the strip of land between South and North Korea. Beyond Imjingak it's a military area: you can only visit the designated sites, on the city's shuttle tour or a tour company's day trip from Seoul.

Nov 23–30, 2026: Paju City says the road to Dora Observatory is fully closed for roadworks. On tour days in that period only the Third Tunnel is open, and tickets are sold on site only. (Nov 23 and Nov 30 are Mondays, when the tour is closed anyway.)

Is the JSA (Panmunjeom) open?

Not for foreign visitors right now. The Ministry of Unification, which runs Panmunjeom visits, says it isn't running tours for foreigners or groups, and will announce any change separately. The Korea Tourism Organization's DMZ page also lists Panmunjeom as closed for civilian tours. The ministry's 2026 white paper notes that limited special visits restarted in 2025 while general visits stayed suspended — so don't assume an ordinary tourist visit is possible. If a tour mentions the JSA, check exactly which sites are included before you book.

Source: Ministry of Unification — Panmunjeom tours (Korean, updated Aug 24, 2026), 2026 Unification White Paper (Korean) and KTO — Regional DMZ attractions; checked Oct 29, 2026.

The DMZ Peace Tour from Imjingak

Paju City runs the tour as a shuttle bus loop: Imjingak → Third Tunnel → Dora Observatory → Unification Village → Imjingak, about 3 hours. There's no guide on board; bring your own if you want one.

Fares (individual, by the city's fee table):

With tunnel monorailWalking down
Adult₩12,200₩9,200
School students (elementary to high school)₩9,500₩7,000

Source: DMZ Peace Tour — fees (Korean); checked Oct 28, 2026.

The adult fare is a facility fee (₩5,000), the monorail (₩3,000) and the shuttle bus (₩4,200). Paju City says foreign visitors aren't eligible for its fee reductions, so plan on the full fare; ask at the ticket office about student fares.

Departures: individual shuttle buses leave Imjingak from 09:20 to 14:40 — 10 departures on weekdays (Tuesday–Friday) and 14 on weekends. Some departures use the monorail into the tunnel and some walk down. Only part of each bus can be booked online (14 of 44 seats on most departures); the rest is sold at Imjingak on the day. If the monorail breaks down, everyone walks.

Source: DMZ Peace Tour — timetable (Korean); checked Oct 28, 2026.

Rules that can stop you at the gate

  • Original ID only. Foreign visitors need a passport, a Korean alien registration card or a US military ID. Copies, phone photos and expired passports aren't accepted. Minors can enter with a guardian.
  • Everyone must be there. Tickets are sold only to people who are actually at Imjingak, once your whole group has arrived.
  • No photos or videos (drones included) anywhere on the tour route.
  • The Third Tunnel is narrow and steep. Older visitors, people with high or low blood pressure or breathing problems, and anyone who has been drinking may be turned away, and visitors with limited mobility may not be able to go inside.
  • It can be cancelled at short notice for security threats, bad weather or safety issues — even if you've booked.

Source: Paju City — DMZ Peace Tour (Korean); checked Oct 28, 2026.

KTO also lists bus 9710 from the Sungnyemun stop near Seoul Station Exit 3 to Munsan (about ₩2,800; up to 1 hour 50 minutes in traffic). Its recommended route is the train to Munsan, then a taxi. Ticket sales stop at 14:30, so aim to reach Imjingak by late morning.

Your own trip or a tour company?

Going on your own is the cheapest way, but there's no guide and tickets can sell out. Tour companies also run DMZ day trips from Seoul; check whether transport, a guide and the tour tickets are included, and which sites you'll actually visit. Either way the same rules apply: passport, no Monday visits, and no JSA tours for foreign visitors at the moment.

Before you go

  1. Pick a day from Tuesday to Sunday that isn't a weekday public holiday — see Korea's public holidays.
  2. Pack your original passport.
  3. Book online if you can (collect the ticket at Imjingak by 14:30), or reach Imjingak well before 14:30.
  4. Visiting Nov 24–29, 2026? Only the Third Tunnel is open (the tour is closed on Mondays).
  5. Bring a transit card for the train to Munsan.

FAQ

Can foreigners visit the DMZ?

Yes. Foreign visitors can join Paju City's DMZ Peace Tour from Imjingak with an original passport. The Joint Security Area (Panmunjeom) isn't open to foreign visitors at the moment.

Is the JSA tour open in 2026?

Not for foreign visitors at the moment. The Ministry of Unification says it isn't running tours for foreigners or groups, and the Korea Tourism Organization lists Panmunjeom as closed for civilian tours. Check the official status before booking any tour that mentions the JSA.

How much does the DMZ tour cost?

Paju City's DMZ Peace Tour costs ₩12,200 for adults with the tunnel monorail, or ₩9,200 if you walk down. Tour companies from Seoul set their own prices.

Is the DMZ tour open on Mondays?

No. It's closed on Mondays, on public holidays that fall on weekdays, and on the main day of Seollal and Chuseok.

Can I take photos at the DMZ?

Not on the tour route — Paju City bans photos and videos, including drones, inside the DMZ Peace Tour area.
