Changdeokgung Palace and the Secret Garden (Huwon): Tickets, Booking and English Tours

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 27, 20266 min read

Quick answer

  • The Secret Garden (Huwon) is a timed, guided visit you book ahead. Online tickets open at 10:00 Korea time, 6 days before your visit (not counting the visit day); some tickets are also sold on the day from 09:00.
  • You need two tickets for the garden: the palace ticket (foreign adults 19–64: ₩3,000) plus the garden ticket (₩5,000 for adults, including 65 and over; ₩2,500 for ages 7–18). Wearing hanbok doesn't make the garden free.
  • Changdeokgung normally closes on Mondays (if a Monday is a public or substitute holiday, it opens that day and closes on the first non-holiday afterward). English garden tours start at 10:30, 11:30, 14:30 and, from March to November, 15:30.

To see Changdeokgung's Secret Garden (Huwon), book a timed entry: online tickets open at 10:00 Korea time six days before your visit (not counting the visit day), and the garden needs its own ₩5,000 adult ticket on top of the ₩3,000 palace ticket for foreign adults (19–64). The palace normally closes on Mondays (if Monday is a public or substitute holiday, it opens that day and closes on the first non-holiday afterward). English garden tours start at 10:30, 11:30 and 14:30, plus 15:30 from March to November.

Changdeokgung is on UNESCO's World Heritage List (1997). Below: hours, the two tickets, how garden booking works, tour times and a simple plan.

Source: Royal Palaces and Tombs Center (Korean) and UNESCO; checked Oct 27, 2026.

How Secret Garden booking works

  1. Buy online from 10:00 Korea time, 6 days before your visit (the visit day isn't counted), up to the day before. Tickets are first come, first served; the palace's reservation page (Korean) links to garden booking.
  2. Or buy on the day from 09:00 at the palace. Each tour has 100 places per language: 50 sold online and 50 on site.
  3. April–May and October–November sell out very fast, both online and on site, the palace office says.
  4. Buy the palace ticket too. A garden ticket alone doesn't get you in.
  5. Be at the garden entrance on time. It's 15 minutes or more on foot from Geumhomun Gate, the palace entrance. If you miss your entry time, you can't enter and can't get a refund.

Source: Royal Palaces and Tombs Center — admission fees and Huwon booking and guided tours (Korean); checked Oct 27, 2026.

Our take: In April–May or October–November, set an alarm for 10:00 Korea time six days before your visit. If you miss out, arrive before 09:00 for the on-site tickets.

Before you go

  1. Check the closing day: usually Monday, with the public/substitute-holiday exception.
  2. Book the Secret Garden online at 10:00, 6 days ahead, or buy on site from 09:00.
  3. Buy both tickets: palace (₩3,000 for foreign adults 19–64) and garden (₩5,000).
  4. Leave 15 minutes or more to walk from the gate to the garden entrance.
  5. Bring ID if you're claiming a free or child ticket by age.

Source: the palace pages above; checked Oct 27, 2026.

FAQ

Do I need to book the Secret Garden at Changdeokgung?

Booking is the safest way. Online tickets open at 10:00 Korea time, 6 days before your visit (not counting the visit day). Half of each tour's 100 places are sold on site from 09:00 on the day, and spring and autumn sell out very fast.

How much is the Changdeokgung Secret Garden ticket?

₩5,000 for adults aged 19 and over (including 65 and over) and ₩2,500 for ages 7–18, plus the palace ticket (₩3,000 for foreign adults 19–64). Children 6 and under are free.

Is the Secret Garden free if I wear hanbok?

No. Hanbok gets you into the palace grounds free, but the garden's free list covers only children 6 and under and certain Korean nationals.

Is there an English tour of the Secret Garden?

Yes, Tuesday to Sunday at 10:30, 11:30 and 14:30, plus 15:30 from March to November. It takes about 70 minutes.

What day is Changdeokgung closed?

Usually Monday. If a Monday is a public or substitute holiday, it opens that day and closes on the first non-holiday afterward.
