Busan Travel Guide: Beaches, Gamcheon Village and Jagalchi Market

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 23, 20266 min read

Quick answer

  • Busan is Korea's big seaside city. Start with Haeundae Beach, Gamcheon Culture Village and Jagalchi Market, one of Korea's largest seafood markets.
  • Get there by KTX: Seoul to Busan via Gyeongju is ₩54,400 (standard adult), or ₩52,900 from Suseo; fares differ by route. In the city, the subway costs ₩1,600 or ₩1,800 by transit card.
  • Eat dwaeji gukbap (pork and rice soup). A Visit Busan food guide puts it first, followed by milmyeon (wheat noodles).

Busan's three starter sights are Haeundae Beach (free, about 1.5 km of sand), Gamcheon Culture Village (free, open daily) and Jagalchi Market (main building closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays). From Seoul Station, the KTX via Gyeongju costs ₩54,400 (adult standard class; fares vary by route), and the city subway costs ₩1,600 or ₩1,800 by transit card. The dish to try is dwaeji gukbap, pork and rice soup.

Haeundae Beach

The Korea Tourism Organization calls Haeundae the most famous beach in Busan, with white sand in front of a shallow bay. It gets crowded every summer.

EntryFree
SizeAbout 1.5 km long and 30–50 m wide
NearbyDongbaekseom Island, Busan Aquarium
SwimmingDepends on the season and on-site rules: check the signs at the beach

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Haeundae Beach; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Gamcheon Culture Village

Refugees settled this hillside during the Korean War. In 2009, students, artists and residents decorated it as part of a village art project, and it became known as "the Machu Picchu of Korea."

EntryFree (parking is charged)
OpenEvery day · 09:00–18:00 (March–October) · 09:00–17:00 (November–February)
Getting thereSubway Line 1 to Toseong Station, Exit 6 → village bus Saha 1-1, Seo-gu 2 or Seo-gu 2-2 → Gamjeong Elementary School, then about a 5-minute walk
DoBuy a stamp map at the village information center and follow it through the alleys

Source: Visit Busan — Gamcheon Culture Village; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Local tip: People live here. Visit Busan asks visitors to keep quiet and keep it clean, and drones aren't allowed.

Jagalchi Market

One of the largest seafood markets in Korea, selling both live and dried fish, and you can eat fresh raw fish right at the market. Most of the sellers are women, known as the Jagalchi ajumeoni.

Hours05:00–22:00, as listed by the Korea Tourism Organization; individual stalls and restaurants keep their own hours
ClosedMain market building: 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays of the month
EatRaw fish and seafood at the market

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Jagalchi Market and Busan Infrastructure Corporation — Jagalchi Market; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Local tip: Dwaeji gukbap is made with pork bones. If you avoid pork or have other dietary needs, ask about the ingredients before you order.

A two-day plan

Our take: Gamcheon (which Visit Busan calls a landmark of west Busan) and Jagalchi in Jung-gu fit well on one day. Give Haeundae its own afternoon and evening.
DayWhere
Day 1Gamcheon Culture Village → Jagalchi Market for a seafood meal
Day 2Haeundae Beach, then dwaeji gukbap for dinner

Before you go

  1. Book your KTX to Busan (Seoul Station or Suseo) and check the fare for your train.
  2. Bring a transit card such as T-money for the subway and village buses.
  3. Install Naver Map for routes between sights.
  4. Check that the Jagalchi main building isn't closed that day (1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays).
  5. Save the Busan travel hotline: 1330.

Source: Busan Infrastructure Corporation — Jagalchi Market and Visit Busan; checked Oct 23, 2026.

FAQ

How do I get from Seoul to Busan?

By KTX. A standard adult ticket from Seoul Station via Gyeongju costs ₩54,400, or ₩52,900 from Suseo Station. Fares differ by route, so check your train when booking.

Is Gamcheon Culture Village free?

Yes. Entry is free (parking is charged), and it's open daily: 09:00–18:00 from March to October and 09:00–17:00 from November to February.

When is Jagalchi Market closed?

The main market building is closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays of the month.

What food is Busan famous for?

A Visit Busan food guide puts dwaeji gukbap (pork and rice soup) first, followed by milmyeon (wheat noodles). Jagalchi Market is known for seafood.

How much is the Busan subway?

₩1,600 or ₩1,800 for an adult with a transit card, depending on distance.
