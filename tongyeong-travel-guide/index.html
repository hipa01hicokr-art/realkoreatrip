Tongyeong Travel Guide: Cable Car, Island Ferries and Chungmu Gimbap

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 30, 2026Updated: Oct 31, 20269 min read

Quick answer

  • Tongyeong is a harbor town for islands, seafood and Admiral Yi Sun-sin's naval history. Ride the cable car up Mireuksan (adults ₩17,000 return), see Sebyeonggwan Hall (adults ₩3,000) and take a ferry to Hansan-do.
  • Get there by express bus: Seoul Express Bus Terminal to Tongyeong takes about 4 hours 10 minutes (adult fare example ₩42,200, Udeung class). The bus details here were checked on Kobus.
  • Bring your passport for island ferries. Foreign passengers show a valid passport (or alien registration card) to board, and you can't board without ID.

Tongyeong, on Korea's south coast about 4 hours 10 minutes from Seoul by express bus, is the base for the islands of the Hallyeosudo waterway and for Admiral Yi Sun-sin's naval history. Start with the cable car up Mireuksan (adults ₩17,000 return), Sebyeonggwan Hall, a National Treasure (adults ₩3,000), and Chungmu gimbap by the harbor, then take a ferry to Hansan-do (adult fare example ₩6,300 one way). Bring your passport: island ferries check ID.

Source: Tongyeong Cable Car (Korean), Tongyeong Hansan Daecheop Cultural Foundation (Korean), Korea Shipping Association (Korean) and Kobus (Korean); checked Oct 30, 2026.

The cable car and Mireuksan

The cable car runs from the Donam Tourist Complex to just below the peak of Mireuksan, with views over the Hallyeosudo waterway. It's 1,975 m long. From the top, Tongyeong City says, you look out over the waters of the Great Battle of Hansan.

Local tip: Go in the morning. Ticket sales can close hours before the cable car stops, the operator says, and it has regular closures on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays (the following day when that Wednesday is a public holiday): check the monthly notices.

Skyline Luge Tongyeong

Skyline Luge Tongyeong has four downhill tracks totaling 3.8 km. A 3-ride ticket is ₩34,000 for an adult. Solo riders must be at least 110 cm tall and 6 years old, subject to staff assessment. Tandem riding has additional age, height and ticket restrictions; meeting the minimum age of 2 and height of 85 cm doesn't by itself establish eligibility. The operator lists an 85–120 cm height band for child doubling tickets and requires a tandem guardian aged 19 or over. Check its detailed height bands and confirm borderline cases with staff before buying a ticket. Show your luge ticket (not just a receipt) at the cable car for ₩2,000 off, within 2 days; with a family ticket the discount covers up to five people, and it can't be combined with other discounts.

Source: Skyline Luge Tongyeong (Korean) and Tongyeong Cable Car — discounts (Korean); checked Oct 30, 2026.

Sebyeonggwan Hall and Tongjeyeong

Tongjeyeong was the headquarters of the Joseon navy's commander of the three southern provinces, in use until 1895. Its main hall, Sebyeonggwan, was first built in 1605 and rebuilt on a larger scale in 1646; it's a National Treasure (designated 2002). Tongyeong City calls it one of the largest Joseon-era buildings by floor area, open-sided, with no walls or windows between its 50 columns, and says the name means "washing weapons in the stream of the Milky Way."

Hansan-do: Admiral Yi's island

Hansan-do is where Admiral Yi Sun-sin set up his naval command during the Japanese invasions: in August 1593 he built the Unjudang there. It was destroyed in 1597, and Jeseungdang, the hall you visit today, was rebuilt on its site in 1740. Tongyeong City's fee list shows Jeseungdang as free.

Local tip: Island ferries check ID when you board, and you can't board without it, even with a mobile or online ticket. Foreign passengers can use a valid passport or an alien registration card, the Korea Shipping Association says.

Gangguan harbor, Dongpirang and the markets

  • Gangguan: the old harbor in the middle of town. Nearby are exhibits of Joseon-era warships, including the Geobukseon (turtle ship) and the panokseon, Tongyeong City says.
  • Dongpirang: a hillside village above Gangguan where the murals along its winding lanes are renewed through painting contests. At the top, the Dongporu pavilion looks over the harbor.
  • Jungang Market: across from Gangguan, a seafood market selling fish caught off Tongyeong, the city says. Seoho Market is the town's other traditional market.

Source: Tongyeong City — Gangguan, markets and Dongpirang (Korean); checked Oct 30, 2026.

Local tip: Dongpirang is a real neighborhood. The city says its shops and cafés close in the evening and asks visitors to keep quiet at night, so go in daylight.

Before you go

  1. Book your express bus from Seoul and plan to stay a night.
  2. Pack your passport for island ferries.
  3. Check the cable car's monthly notices: regular closures fall on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays (the next day when that Wednesday is a public holiday).
  4. Check Hansan-do sailings on the Korea Shipping Association site the day before.
  5. Visit Sebyeonggwan Hall well before closing: the official winter hours differ (17:00 or 18:00), so confirm the cutoff.

Source: Tongyeong Cable Car (Korean), Korea Shipping Association (Korean) and Tongyeong Hansan Daecheop Cultural Foundation (Korean); checked Oct 30, 2026.

FAQ

How do I get from Seoul to Tongyeong?

By express bus from Seoul Express Bus Terminal, about 4 hours 10 minutes. An adult Udeung-class fare example is ₩42,200; premium buses cost more.

Is there a train to Tongyeong?

For this trip, plan on the express bus from Seoul: the city's transport guide lists bus routes, and the bus times and fares here were checked on Kobus.

How much is the Tongyeong cable car?

Adults pay ₩17,000 return or ₩13,500 one way. It has regular closures on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays (the next day if that Wednesday is a public holiday) and can stop in bad weather.

Do I need a passport for the ferry to Hansan-do?

You need an accepted ID: a valid passport is the simplest option for short-term visitors, and an alien registration card also works. Island ferries check ID when you board.

Is Tongyeong worth visiting?

In our view, yes, for island views, Admiral Yi's naval history and harbor food like Chungmu gimbap. Stay at least one night: the bus from Seoul takes about 4 hours.
