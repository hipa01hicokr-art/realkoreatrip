Andong Travel Guide: Hahoe Village, Confucian Academies and Jjimdak

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 30, 20268 min read

Quick answer

  • Andong is where you see old Korea's clan villages and Confucian academies. Sites in Andong belong to three UNESCO listings: Hahoe Village (2010), Dosan and Byeongsan Seowon (2019) and Bongjeongsa Temple (2018).
  • Hahoe Village costs ₩5,000 for adults. Last entry is 16:30 from October to March and 17:30 from April to September, so don't arrive late in the afternoon.
  • Get there by KTX-Eum from Seoul Station, Cheongnyangni or (on selected trains) Sangbong to Andong Station. Journey times depend on the departure station, so check your train when booking, then take bus 210 or Express 2 to Hahoe.

Andong, reached from Seoul by KTX-Eum, is the place to see a living clan village and the Confucian academies of the Joseon era: sites here belong to three UNESCO World Heritage listings. Start with Hahoe Village (adults ₩5,000; last entry 16:30 from October to March), the Hahoe mask dance show at 14:00 (Tuesday to Sunday from March to December, Saturdays and Sundays in January and February) and a plate of Andong jjimdak, a braised chicken dish born in the city's old market.

Source: UNESCO, Andong Hahoe Village (Korean), Hahoe mask dance society (Korean) and Korea Tourism Organization; checked Oct 30, 2026.

Why Andong matters

UNESCO describes Hahoe and its sister village Yangdong as "the two most representative historic, clan villages in Korea," founded in the 14th–15th century. Andong's sites are part of three World Heritage listings:

Hahoe Village

The Nakdonggang River wraps around Hahoe, and the Ryu clan of Pungsan still makes up 70 percent of the villagers, the Korea Tourism Organization says. UNESCO notes the clusters of one-storey, mud-walled, thatched-roof houses that were "formerly for commoners." A full walk takes about 1 to 1.5 hours.

Local tip: Hahoe's free admission for people aged 65 and over applies to Korean nationals, the village's own fee table says, so older foreign visitors should ask at the ticket office which fare applies.

The Hahoe mask dance show

Hahoe Byeolsingut Tallori, a mask dance drama performed in carved wooden Hahoe masks, is a National Intangible Cultural Heritage (designated 1980). The preservation society stages a regular one-hour show at the village's mask dance theater:

MonthsDaysTime
March to DecemberTuesday to Sunday14:00–15:00
January and FebruarySaturday and Sunday14:00–15:00

Source: Hahoe Byeolsingut Tallori Preservation Society (Korean) and Korea Tourism Organization; checked Oct 30, 2026.

Local tip: The 14:00 mask dance runs Tuesday to Sunday from March to December and on Saturdays and Sundays in January and February, once a day, so build your Hahoe visit around it. Check the society's current schedule before you go.

Seonyu Julbul Nori

Hahoe also holds Seonyu Julbul Nori on select Saturday nights. The published 2026 schedule lists selected Saturdays in May and August–October, ending on October 31. For future visits, check the organizer's newly announced dates and ticket rules; don't assume the 2026 booking arrangements will apply in 2027.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Andong and Andong Hahoe Village notices (Korean); checked Oct 30, 2026.

Before you go

  1. Book your KTX-Eum and check the new timetable (from Nov 1, 2026).
  2. Plan to reach Hahoe well before last entry (16:30 from October to March).
  3. Catch the 14:00 mask dance show: Tuesday to Sunday from March to December; Saturdays and Sundays in January and February.
  4. Check bus times on Naver Map or the Andong BIS: some routes run a few times a day.
  5. Coming as a pair or group? Order a whole jjimdak to share.

FAQ

Is Andong worth visiting?

In our view, yes, if you want old Korea beyond the palaces of Seoul: Hahoe is a clan village where people still live, and Andong's sites are part of three UNESCO World Heritage listings.

How do I get from Seoul to Andong?

Take the KTX-Eum from Seoul Station, Cheongnyangni or (on selected trains) Sangbong to Andong Station. Journey times depend on the departure station; check your train's time and fare when booking, as Korail's timetable changes from Nov 1, 2026.

How much is Hahoe Village?

Adults (19–64) pay ₩5,000, teenagers ₩2,500 and children ₩1,500. Last entry is 16:30 from October to March and 17:30 from April to September.

When is the Hahoe mask dance performance?

At 14:00, Tuesday to Sunday from March to December, and on Saturdays and Sundays in January and February. There's no show on Mondays.

Can you visit Andong in a day trip?

Yes for Hahoe Village, Byeongsan Seowon and Woryeonggyo Bridge, the Korea Tourism Organization says. Stay overnight if you also want Dosan Seowon and Bongjeongsa Temple.
