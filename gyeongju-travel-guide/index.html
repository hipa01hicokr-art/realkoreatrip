Gyeongju Travel Guide: Korea's Ancient Silla Capital in One Day

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 23, 20266 min read

Quick answer

  • Gyeongju was the capital of the Silla kingdom for nearly a thousand years. Two UNESCO listings cover its Silla heritage: the Gyeongju Historic Areas and Seokguram Grotto with Bulguksa Temple.
  • Get there by KTX from Seoul Station (adult standard-class fare example ₩44,500; fares vary by route), then use Naver Map for buses or taxis between sites.
  • Save Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond for the evening. It's open 09:00–22:00 (last entry 21:30); adults pay ₩3,000.

Gyeongju was the capital of the Silla kingdom (57 BCE–935 CE), and its Silla sites are covered by two UNESCO World Heritage listings (1995 and 2000). Start with four sights: Bulguksa Temple, the Daereungwon tombs, Cheomseongdae and Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (open until 22:00, adults ₩3,000). From Seoul Station, the KTX adult standard-class fare example is ₩44,500.

The Korea Tourism Organization calls Gyeongju "a living museum." Below: each sight in a table, English guiding, transport and a one-day plan.

Why Gyeongju matters

UNESCO dates the Silla dynasty from 57 BCE to 935 CE, and says the sites in and around Gyeongju "bear outstanding testimony" to its achievements. This guide focuses on the two World Heritage listings tied to Silla:

UNESCO listingInscribedWhat it covers
Gyeongju Historic Areas2000Five areas in and around the city: royal tombs, the old palace grounds with Cheomseongdae and Wolji Pond, and Buddhist sites on Mount Namsan
Seokguram Grotto and Bulguksa Temple1995An 8th-century grotto with a monumental stone Buddha looking toward the sea, and Bulguksa Temple, built in 774

Gyeongju also has a part in two other listings: Yangdong Village belongs to the Historic Villages of Korea (2010), and Oksan Seowon is one of the Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies (2019).

Source: UNESCO — Gyeongju Historic Areas, Seokguram Grotto and Bulguksa Temple, Hahoe and Yangdong and Seowon; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Where to start

Bulguksa Temple

The Korea Tourism Organization calls Bulguksa one of Korea's most iconic Buddhist temples.

Built774
Don't missThe Seokgatap and Dabotap pagodas (national treasures)
NearbySeokguram Grotto, on the slopes of Mount Toham, listed with it by UNESCO
WhereOn the edge of the city: check the bus or taxi route in Naver Map

Daereungwon Tomb Complex and Cheomseongdae

In the middle of town, the Silla royal tombs rise as rounded green mounds. UNESCO notes that excavations revealed rich grave goods of gold, glass and fine ceramics.

Go insideCheonmachong, the tomb in Daereungwon open to the public, displays Silla-era artifacts including a golden crown
Walk on toCheomseongdae Observatory, part of the same Wolseong area as the old palace site and Wolji Pond

Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond

A secondary palace of the Silla court, used for banquets and important guests. The pond was dug in 674; its name, Wolji, means "a pond that reflects the moon."

Open09:00–22:00, all year (last entry 21:30)
AdmissionAdults ₩3,000 · teenagers ₩2,000 · children ₩1,000
Best timeSunset, when the pavilions are lit and reflected in the water

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Gyeongju course and Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond; UNESCO; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Local tip: Admission at temples and tomb sites has changed in recent years, and figures online often disagree. Check each site's official visitor information before you go instead of relying on an old blog post.

English guiding

Walk-in cultural guiding is offered at major heritage sites, including Bulguksa, Daereungwon and Donggung Palace, from the "Korean Culture & Tourism Guide" office near each entrance.

  • When: sessions generally start on the hour from 10:00 to 16:00, with a lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00. Schedules can change.
  • How: ask at the guide office for that day's English session. No reservation is needed.
  • Bulguksa on Sundays: the city's regular guide roster doesn't cover Bulguksa on Sundays, so confirm there in advance.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Gyeongju course and Gyeongju City's cultural guide information; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Before you go

  1. Book your KTX from Seoul Station to Gyeongju and check the fare for your train.
  2. Install Naver Map for buses and taxis between sites.
  3. Plan Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond for the evening (last entry 21:30).
  4. Bring a little cash and a card.
  5. Want an English guide? Ask at the guide office near the site entrance; at Bulguksa on a Sunday, confirm in advance.

FAQ

Why is Gyeongju famous?

It was the capital of the Silla kingdom for nearly a thousand years. Its Silla heritage is covered by two UNESCO listings: the Gyeongju Historic Areas and Seokguram Grotto with Bulguksa Temple.

How do I get from Seoul to Gyeongju?

By KTX from Seoul Station. An adult standard-class fare example is ₩44,500; fares vary by route, so check the fare of the train you choose when booking.

Is Gyeongju worth a day trip?

In our view, a full day covers Bulguksa, the royal tombs and Donggung Palace at night. Staying overnight gives you more time for Seokguram and the other historic areas.

Are there English tours in Gyeongju?

Yes, walk-in guiding is offered at major heritage sites. Sessions generally start on the hour from 10:00 to 16:00 (not 12:00–13:00); ask at the guide office for that day's English session.

What time does Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond close?

It's open from 09:00 to 22:00, with last entry at 21:30.
