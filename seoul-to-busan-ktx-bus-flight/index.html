Seoul to Busan: KTX vs Express Bus vs Flight

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 29, 20266 min read

Quick answer

  • KTX train: ₩54,400 for an adult in standard class from Seoul Station. Journey times depend on the train — check yours when you book; the Korea Tourism Organization puts the trip at about 3 hours.
  • Express bus: about 4 hours from Seoul Express Bus Terminal. Adult fares are ₩29,100 (Economy), ₩43,300 (Excellent) and ₩56,200 (Premium) after a 9% rise on October 1, 2026.
  • Flight: Gimpo to Gimhae is scheduled at about 65–75 minutes from departure to arrival, but add the trips to and from both airports and security. Fares vary by airline and date.

The KTX links the two city centers fastest: ₩54,400 for an adult in standard class, and about 3 hours according to the Korea Tourism Organization (times depend on the train). The express bus is cheaper — ₩29,100 in Economy class (only a few evening departures), or ₩43,300 for the roomier Excellent class — but takes about 4 hours. A flight from Gimpo to Gimhae is scheduled at about 65–75 minutes, but both airports are outside the center.

Source: Korail (Korean), Kobus timetable and Korea Airports Corporation (Korean); checked Oct 29, 2026.

Our take: Take the KTX unless saving money matters most — then take the Excellent-class bus. Flying may not save much time from central Seoul once you add the airport trips and security, but it can make sense if you're staying near Gimpo or heading to west Busan.

KTX: fastest from city center to city center

  • Fare: ₩54,400 for an adult in standard class from Seoul Station (via Gyeongju), or ₩52,900 from Suseo Station in southeast Seoul. Fares differ by route.
  • Time: it depends on the train and its stops, so check the journey time for your train when you book. The Korea Tourism Organization puts the trip at about 3 hours. In September 2024, Korail reported express services stopping only at Daejeon and Dongdaegu that took 2 hours 17 minutes — a past figure, not a check of today's timetable.
  • Arrival: Busan Station is in the city center, on Busan Metro Line 1.

Source: Korail — express KTX, Sep 2, 2024 and KTX–SRT merger, Aug 5, 2026 (Korean); Korea Tourism Organization; checked Oct 29, 2026.

How to book, when tickets go on sale and the Korail Pass are in our KTX booking guide.

Express bus: cheapest

Buses to Busan leave from Seoul Express Bus Terminal (the Gyeongbu building, in Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu). For travel on Thursday, November 5, 2026 (we checked the timetable on October 29), there were 32 departures, from 01:00 to midnight, each scheduled at 4 hours.

  • Fares went up 9% on October 1, 2026. Older blog posts show the old prices.
  • Dong Seoul Terminal (east Seoul) also has a few buses — 3 that day, Excellent class only, ₩43,500, about 4 hours 15 minutes.
  • Arrival: the Busan terminal is in Nopo-dong, Geumjeong-gu, in the north of the city — the Korea Tourism Organization calls it Busan Central Bus Terminal.
  • Luggage: the bus company's terms allow one carry-on item (10 kg or less, or under 40,000 cm³) and one item in the hold (under 30 kg, or under 40,000 cm³) per person for free. Dangerous or specially restricted items follow separate rules in the same terms.

Source: Kobus notices of Sep 22, 2026 (fares) and Feb 24, 2026 (payment), terminal information and terms of carriage (Korean); checked Oct 29, 2026.

Booking the bus

Kobus, the official express bus site, has an English version. Two things to know:

  • Since February 24, 2026, people booking on the website without an account can no longer type in a card number; the site lists only Toss Pay or Naver Pay, which are Korean payment apps.
  • Kobus's English page points visitors to Klook for booking in their own language.

For using foreign cards and cash in Korea in general, see our payment guide.

Flight: Gimpo to Gimhae

  • Flight time: the winter schedule (from October 25, 2026) shows about 65–75 minutes between scheduled departure and arrival — not measured time in the air. First and last flights vary by date and weekday (the earliest listed departures are around 06:50–07:00, the latest around 20:10), so check the airport or airline schedule for your day. Airlines can change times.
  • Bring your passport. At the departure entrance, you show ID and your boarding pass; the airport lists a passport as accepted ID.
  • Getting to Gimpo: see our Gimpo Airport guide — the AREX and Lines 5 and 9 all stop there.
  • From Gimhae into Busan: the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail connects with Busan Metro Line 2 at Sasang (about 6 minutes) and Line 3 at Daejeo. Limousine buses run to Seomyeon (₩6,000) and Haeundae (₩9,500), card only.

Source: Korea Airports Corporation — Gimhae schedule, boarding guide and transport (Korean); Korea Tourism Organization; checked Oct 29, 2026.

FAQ

How long is the KTX from Seoul to Busan?

About 3 hours, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, but it depends on the train — check your train's journey time when you book. (Korail reported 2 hours 17 minutes for express services in 2024; that's a past figure, not a current timetable.)

How much is the bus from Seoul to Busan?

From Seoul Express Bus Terminal, an adult pays ₩29,100 (Economy), ₩43,300 (Excellent) or ₩56,200 (Premium), on Kobus's timetable after the 9% fare rise on October 1, 2026. The trip takes about 4 hours.

Is it faster to fly from Seoul to Busan?

The scheduled time is short: about 65–75 minutes from departure at Gimpo to arrival at Gimhae. But both airports are outside the city centers and you need time for security, so compare the whole door-to-door trip with the KTX before you book.

Where does the bus from Seoul arrive in Busan?

At the bus terminal in Nopo-dong, Geumjeong-gu, in the north of Busan, which the Korea Tourism Organization calls Busan Central Bus Terminal.

Can I take big luggage on the express bus?

The bus terms allow one item in the hold (under 30 kg) and one carry-on (10 kg or less) per person for free.
