Incheon Airport to Seoul: AREX Train vs Airport Bus vs Taxi

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 22, 20268 min read

Quick answer

  • Cheapest: AREX All-stop train with a T-money card — about ₩4,750 (T1) or ₩5,350 (T2) to Seoul Station, around 1 hour.
  • Fastest to Seoul Station: AREX Express — ₩13,000; 43 min between Seoul Station and T1, 51 min to T2. Buy a separate ticket before boarding.
  • With heavy luggage: airport limousine bus (₩16,000–₩17,000 for adults on Airport Limousine's Seoul routes; other operators vary) or a taxi. Arriving late? Night buses (route numbers start with N) and taxis run when trains and regular buses have finished.
Recent changes to know
  • Oct 23, 2026From October 23, 2026, AREX lists 56 Express services a day in total across both directions. The timetable revision also affects some All-stop departures. Check the current timetable for your travel date, terminal and direction.

Of the four options compared here, the least expensive way from Incheon Airport into Seoul is the AREX All-stop train: ₩4,750 from Terminal 1 or ₩5,350 from Terminal 2 to Seoul Station for an adult with a transit card, about 59 or 66 minutes. The AREX Express is the fastest to Seoul Station (₩13,000; 43 minutes from T1, 51 from T2). With heavy luggage, an airport limousine bus (₩16,000–₩17,000 for adults on Airport Limousine's Seoul routes; other operators vary) or a taxi is easier.

Source: AREX All-stop fares, AREX Express and Airport Limousine; checked Oct 22, 2026.

Option 1: AREX All-stop train (cheapest)

The All-stop train is a regular commuter train that runs from the airport into Seoul, stopping at stations like Gimpo Airport, Digital Media City, Hongik Univ. (Hongdae), Gongdeok and Seoul Station.

  • Pay with T-money — just tap in at the gate. (If you don't have a card yet, see how to get a T-money card. Single-journey cards cost ₩100 more, plus a ₩500 deposit you get back at your destination.)
  • Fares from T1 / T2 (adult, transit card):

  • Time: about 59 minutes between Seoul Station and T1, and 66 minutes to T2 (some trains take 2–6 minutes longer).
  • Check the current AREX timetable for the last All-stop train from your terminal to your destination. AREX's timetable revision effective October 23, 2026 also affects some All-stop departures.
  • You can transfer to the Seoul subway at stations like Hongik Univ., Gongdeok and Seoul Station.
Local tip: If you're staying around Hongdae or Mapo, the All-stop train gets you there without changing — and it's cheaper than the Express, which only goes to Seoul Station.

Option 2: AREX Express train (fastest to Seoul Station)

The Express connects Seoul Station, Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. It runs non-stop between Terminal 1 and Seoul Station; trains from Terminal 2 call at Terminal 1 first.

  • Fare: ₩13,000 for adults, ₩9,500 for children (ages 6–12).
  • Time: 43 minutes between Seoul Station and T1, 51 minutes to T2 (time on the train, not including walking or waiting).
  • You need a separate Express ticket — buy it before boarding at the Express Train Customer Center (B1 of the Transportation Center at T1 and T2) or online on the AREX website in advance.
  • AREX's revised Express timetable is effective from October 23, 2026. Check the official timetable for your travel date, departure terminal and direction before planning around a train.

Source: AREX Express, checked Oct 22, 2026.

Our take: The Express makes most sense if your hotel is near Seoul Station or you're catching a KTX train from there. For most other neighborhoods, you'll still need to transfer to the subway, so the time saved over the All-stop train is smaller than it looks.

Option 3: Airport limousine bus

Limousine buses run from the airport to many parts of Seoul. Check the route list for the stop closest to where you're staying.

  • Fare (Airport Limousine's Seoul routes): adults ₩17,000 on direct routes and ₩16,000 on routes via Gimpo Airport; children (elementary-school age) ₩12,000. Other bus operators' fares may differ.
  • Buy a ticket before boarding at the bus ticket counters or ticket machines. Don't count on paying cash or T-money on the bus when leaving the airport.
  • Going back to the airport? Airport Limousine says you can pay with cash or T-money on its buses in that direction (not with a regular credit card or a Climate Card). Other operators may differ.
  • T1 ticket counters: 1st floor arrivals hall, near the exit gates.
  • T2 ticket counters: B1, Transportation Center.
  • Ticket machines run 24 hours.
  • Journey time depends on the route and traffic.

Source: Airport Limousine — fares, ticket counters and payment FAQ (Korean), checked Oct 22, 2026.

Our take: If there's a limousine stop near your hotel, the bus saves you from carrying big suitcases through subway transfers. Check the walking distance from the stop to your hotel before you choose it.

Option 4: Taxi

Use the official taxi stands outside the arrivals hall (1st floor at both terminals). The airport warns travelers not to use touts who approach you inside the terminal. There are separate stands for Seoul, Gyeonggi and Incheon taxis — for central Seoul, follow the signs to the Seoul taxi stand. The rates below are for Seoul taxis.

Metered taxis (Seoul rates):

  • Tolls are added on top of the meter.
  • Late-night surcharge (regular taxis): +20% from 10 pm to 11 pm, +40% from 11 pm to 2 am, +20% from 2 am to 4 am. Deluxe taxis add 20% from 10 pm to 4 am.

International Taxi: International Taxi has fixed zone fares between Incheon Airport and Seoul — ₩70,000–₩95,000 for a sedan and ₩100,000–₩140,000 for an SUV, van or deluxe car, depending on the zone of your destination. Book at least 24 hours ahead; for help on the day, ask at an airport information desk.

Source: Seoul Metropolitan Government — International taxi section fares and International Taxi, checked Oct 22, 2026.

Our take: For a group of three or four with luggage, a taxi is worth pricing out — four adult limousine tickets already cost ₩64,000–₩68,000, and a taxi drops you at your address. With four people and big suitcases, check that the car is large enough. For one or two people, the train or bus is much cheaper.

Landing late at night?

Check the last train and regular airport bus for your terminal and destination before you fly — last departures differ by route and terminal. If those services have finished:

  • Night buses run from the airport to Seoul. Their route numbers start with "N" (for example N6000, N6002 and N6701 from T1), and adult fares on these routes are ₩17,000–₩18,000. Buy tickets in advance at the bus ticket counters or machines. Departure times are limited, so check the airport's late-night bus timetable for your terminal before you fly (T1 · T2, Korean).
  • Taxis wait at the official stands — remember the late-night surcharge.

Source: Incheon Airport — late-night buses from T1 (Korean) and Korea Tourism Organization, checked Oct 22, 2026.

Using a Climate Card pass from the airport?

Seoul's Climate Card short-term pass (unlimited Seoul subway and bus rides) lets you get off at the Incheon Airport T1 and T2 stations, but you can't board the train there with it. This has been the rule since September 13, 2024, and Seoul restated it in its October 1, 2026 coverage update. So you can use it on your way to the airport, but not for your first ride into Seoul — pay for that ride with T-money or a ticket.

Source: Seoul Metropolitan Government — Climate Card and Climate Card FAQ (Korean, updated Oct 1, 2026), checked Oct 22, 2026.

FAQ

Can I use T-money on the AREX Express?

The Express requires a separate ticket bought before boarding. T-money works on the All-stop train.

Does the train stop at Gimpo Airport?

Only the All-stop train stops at Gimpo Airport. The Express serves Terminal 2, Terminal 1 and Seoul Station, running non-stop between Terminal 1 and Seoul Station.

How do I get between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2?

Take the free terminal shuttle bus (route 03 from T2 to T1, every 7–20 minutes, 4:30 am–11:35 pm; route 04 from T1 to T2, every 10–20 minutes, 4:30 am–11:20 pm), or the AREX train (about 6 minutes; ₩1,200 for adults with a transit card, ₩1,300 with a single-journey card, plus a ₩500 deposit you get back).

I'm stuck — who can help in English?

Call the 1330 Korea Travel Helpline (+82-2-1330 from a foreign phone) for help in English and several other languages, or ask at the airport information desks in the arrivals hall.
