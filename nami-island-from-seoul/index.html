Nami Island from Seoul: Tickets, Ferry Times and How to Get There

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 29, 2026Updated: Oct 31, 20265 min read

Quick answer

  • Admission is ₩19,000 for adults, including the return ferry (about 5 minutes each way). It's ₩16,000 for middle and high school students and people 70 and over, and ₩13,000 for ages 36 months to elementary school.
  • Early or late tickets are ₩13,000: early-bird means you buy your ticket AND board the ferry before 08:00; late tickets are bought from 18:01 (Dec–Mar) or from 18:31 (Apr–Nov). Regular ferries to the island run 08:00–21:00, with a seasonal extension in autumn 2026.
  • Getting there: the ITX-Cheongchun train from Yongsan (about 60 min) or Cheongnyangni (about 42 min) to Gapyeong Station, then a 20-minute walk or about 5 minutes by bus 10-4 or taxi to the ferry dock. A direct shuttle bus also runs daily from Hongdae, Myeong-dong and Dongdaemun.

A Nami Island ticket costs ₩19,000 for adults and includes the return ferry, a ride of about 5 minutes from the dock in Gapyeong; ferries to the island run from 08:00 to 21:00, every day of the year. From Seoul, take the ITX-Cheongchun train from Yongsan to Gapyeong (about 60 minutes), then walk 20 minutes or take a 5-minute bus or taxi to the dock.

Source: Nami Island — fees, ferry times and getting here (Korean); checked Oct 28, 2026.

Nami Island is in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province, but you reach it from the Nami Island ferry dock in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi Province — that's the address to put in your map app.

  • Under 36 months: free with a guardian, on the regular ferry.
  • Groups: the operator gives 10% off the total for groups with at least 20 standard-admission guests, using the regular ferry in and out. Confirm eligibility at the ticket office.
  • The motorboat leaves when at least 2 people board and takes up to 5 per boat. It runs 09:00–18:00, seasonally (around April to November).
  • The island brands itself the "Naminara Republic" and sells a one-year "passport" membership (adult ₩69,000) for unlimited visits by the regular ferry.

Extra autumn ferries in 2026 (Sep 18 – Nov 22): the first boat is at 07:30 every day, and extra boats run until 21:30 on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. Times can change in busy seasons — spring flowers, summer holidays and autumn leaves.

From Gapyeong Station the ferry dock is about 1.5 km: about 20 minutes on foot, or about 5 minutes by city bus 10-4 or taxi. The subway works with a transit card; for booking Korail trains, see our KTX booking guide.

By direct shuttle bus

A daily return shuttle bus runs between Seoul and the island, operated by Ktourstory (book on its site):

By car

Search "Nami Island ticket office / ferry dock" or the address 1024 Bukhangangbyeon-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do. Parking costs ₩6,000 for a car for the first 12 hours (₩1,000 per extra hour), card only.

Local tip: The whole island is non-smoking (it was certified a UNICEF Child Friendly Park in 2010), except in designated smoking areas (Gureum Dongsan, "Cloud Hill"). Bikes, e-scooters and kick scooters are hard to take on the ferry, so the island asks you to use its own bike center or tour bus instead, according to its visitor notice (Korean).

Before you go

  1. Head for the ferry dock in Gapyeong, not "Chuncheon."
  2. Check the last ferry back — 21:05 normally.
  3. Taking the shuttle bus? Book ahead; it leaves Hongdae at 09:00.
  4. Driving? Bring a card for parking.

FAQ

How much is the Nami Island ticket?

₩19,000 for adults, including the return ferry. Middle and high school students and people 70 and over pay ₩16,000; children from 36 months to elementary school pay ₩13,000. Under-36-month-olds are free with a guardian on the regular ferry.

How do I get to Nami Island from Seoul?

Take the ITX-Cheongchun train from Yongsan (about 60 minutes) or Cheongnyangni (about 42 minutes) to Gapyeong Station, then walk about 20 minutes or take bus 10-4 or a taxi (about 5 minutes) to the ferry dock. A direct shuttle bus also runs daily from Hongdae, Myeong-dong and Dongdaemun.

What time is the last ferry from Nami Island?

Usually 21:05. During the 2026 autumn extension (Sep 18 – Nov 22), extra boats run until 21:30 on Fridays to Sundays and public holidays.

Is there a cheaper Nami Island ticket?

Yes — early-bird tickets (bought and boarded before 08:00), or late tickets bought from 18:01 from December to March and from 18:31 from April to November, cost ₩13,000.

Is Nami Island open every day?

Yes, the island says it's open 365 days a year.
