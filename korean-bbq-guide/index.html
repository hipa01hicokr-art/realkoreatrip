Korean BBQ for First-Timers: How to Grill, Wrap and Order

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 26, 20264 min read

Quick answer

  • At a Korean BBQ restaurant (gogi-jip), you grill the meat at your table. Some places have staff grill it for you; most tables have a vent that pulls up the smoke.
  • Start with samgyeopsal (grilled pork belly), which the Korea Tourism Organization calls the most popular Korean meat dish. Eat it with salt and sesame oil, or wrapped in lettuce.
  • To make a wrap (ssam), put meat on lettuce or a perilla leaf, add ssamjang sauce and eat it in one bite. Finish with soybean paste stew or fried rice.

At a Korean BBQ restaurant, called a gogi-jip ("meat restaurant"), you grill meat at your table, wrap it in lettuce or a perilla leaf with ssamjang sauce, and eat each wrap in one bite. The Korea Tourism Organization calls samgyeopsal (grilled pork belly) the most popular Korean meat dish. Most tables have a vent for the smoke, and at some places staff grill the meat for you.

Below: what to order, how to make a wrap, how to finish the meal and how to get help.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — About Korean Food (page dated Jul 25, 2025); checked Oct 26, 2026.

How to make a wrap (ssam)

  1. Take a lettuce or perilla leaf.
  2. Add a piece of grilled meat.
  3. Add ssamjang, a sauce of red chili paste and soybean paste mixed with garlic, scallions and sesame oil.
  4. Open wide and eat it all in one bite: that's the Korea Tourism Organization's tip.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — About Korean Food; checked Oct 26, 2026.

Our take: If one bite feels like a lot, use a smaller leaf or less meat. The Korea Tourism Organization's point is to eat each wrap whole rather than biting it in half.

Practical tips

What to know
Calling staffSay "Yeogiyo!" (Excuse me!) to order more or ask a question; if there's a call bell, press it
TippingNot needed; extra water or complimentary side dishes don't cost more (anything you order separately does)
OrderingMenus vary: ask staff how portions are sold, and whether they'll grill or you should
Smoke and smellsMost tables have a vent; some chairs open up to store your coat away from food smells

FAQ

How do you eat Korean BBQ?

Grill the meat at your table (or let staff do it), put it on a lettuce or perilla leaf with ssamjang sauce, and eat the wrap in one bite. Finish with soybean paste stew or fried rice.

What is samgyeopsal?

Grilled pork belly. The Korea Tourism Organization calls it the most popular Korean meat dish and suggests eating it with chili-soybean paste, or sesame oil and salt, or wrapped in lettuce.

What's the difference between galbi and bulgogi?

Galbi is ribs, usually marinated in soy sauce and grilled; pork galbi is a more affordable choice than beef. Bulgogi is thinly sliced, marinated beef that is pan-fried, nowadays with broth or water added.

Do I have to grill the meat myself?

Not always. At some Korean BBQ restaurants, staff grill the meat for you and tell you when it's ready to eat.

Do you tip at Korean BBQ restaurants?

No. The Korea Tourism Organization says there's no need to tip at Korean restaurants.
