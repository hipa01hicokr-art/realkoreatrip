Jeju Island Travel Guide: Volcanoes, Hallasan and Black Pork

By Real Korea Trip Editors✓ Last checked: Oct 23, 20267 min read

Quick answer

  • Jeju is Korea's volcanic island. UNESCO lists Seongsan Ilchulbong, Mount Halla (Hallasan) and the Geomunoreum lava tubes as a World Natural Heritage site (2007).
  • Hiking to the top of Hallasan? Book, bring ID and start early. The two summit trails, Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa, run on reservations, and summit-route checkpoints close at 11:30 (Oct–Mar) or 12:30 (Apr–Sep).
  • Get around by bus with a transit card: ₩1,150 on regular buses; express buses start at ₩2,000 and charge the rest when you tap off.

Jeju is a volcanic island whose three main natural sites, Seongsan Ilchulbong, Hallasan and the Geomunoreum lava tubes, have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2007. The Seongsan Ilchulbong summit trail costs ₩5,000 for adults; the coastal walking area is free. Reaching the top of Hallasan, South Korea's highest mountain, takes a reservation, ID that matches it and an early start: summit-route checkpoints close at 11:30 (October–March) or 12:30 (April–September). Adult fares by transit card are ₩1,150 on regular buses, including tourist circular buses, while express buses start at ₩2,000 and cost up to ₩3,000 depending on distance.

Why Jeju matters

In 2007, UNESCO listed Jeju Volcanic Island and Lava Tubes as a World Heritage Site. It has three parts:

PartWhat UNESCO says
Seongsan IlchulbongA "fortress-like" tuff cone rising out of the ocean
Mount Halla (Hallasan)"The highest in Korea," with a lake-filled crater at the top
Geomunoreum"The finest lava tube system of caves anywhere"

Jeju is also known for its haenyeo, women divers who gather shellfish such as abalone and sea urchins without oxygen masks. UNESCO added the Culture of Jeju Haenyeo to its Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2016.

Source: UNESCO — Jeju Volcanic Island and Lava Tubes and Culture of Jeju Haenyeo; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Seongsan Ilchulbong

Seongsan Ilchulbong rose 180 meters out of the sea more than 5,000 years ago and was once a separate island. The crater at the top is about 600 meters across and 90 meters deep. From the summit you can see Udo Island and watch the sunrise.

AdmissionAdults ₩5,000 · teenagers and children ₩2,500 (paid summit trail)
Free optionA free walking trail around the base and coast
ClimbAbout 30–40 minutes to the summit, on stairs and slopes
ClosedFirst Monday of the month (the next day if that Monday is a holiday)
ParkingFree

Source: Korea Tourism Organization — Seongsan Ilchulbong Tuff Cone and Visit Jeju — Seongsan Ilchulbong; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Local tip: Opening hours vary by season. Before a sunrise visit, check the current opening, last-entry and closing information on Visit Jeju's Seongsan Ilchulbong page.

Hiking Hallasan

The two trails that reach the summit, Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa, run on a reservation system, and you can only enter at your booked time.

Summit trails (Seongpanak, Gwaneumsa)
Bookvisithalla.jeju.go.kr (English menu) · bookings for a month open at 09:00 Korea time on the first business day of the month before
Your ticketEntry and exit QR codes, sent to the phone number you enter
BringID that matches your booking details, plus your QR code
Earliest entry05:00
Uphill cutoff11:30 (October–March) · 12:30 (April–September) at the trail entrance and at the summit-route checkpoints (Jindallaebat on Seongpanak, Samgakbong on Gwaneumsa)
Leave the summit by13:30 (October–March) · 14:30 (April–September)
WeatherTrails close partly or fully during heavy rain, strong wind, typhoon or heavy snow warnings

Don't treat the cutoff as a starting time. The checkpoints are well up the trail, so book an early entry slot and give yourself enough time to reach them.

Rules that can cost you a booking:

  • If you don't cancel and don't show up, you can't book for 3 months (1 year for a second no-show).
  • If your ID doesn't match your QR details, you can be refused entry and banned from booking for 1 year. Reselling QR codes is not allowed.

Source: Hallasan National Park — reservation system, reservation guide, entry and descent time limits and ID check notice; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Local tip: Drinking alcohol and smoking are prohibited in the park, and disposable lunch boxes aren't allowed in. Gimbap and burgers are fine, according to the park's own guide.

Getting around by bus

Jeju's buses reach most of the island, and Visit Jeju recommends a transportation card such as T-money (see our T-money guide).

BusAdult fare by card
Regular buses (including tourist circular buses)₩1,150 (₩1,200 in cash)
Express buses₩2,000 for the first 20 km, up to ₩3,000

On express buses, tap your card again when you get off: the rest of the fare for the distance you rode is charged then. Plan each route in Naver Map.

Source: Jeju Bus Information System — fares and Visit Jeju — Public Transportation in Jeju; checked Oct 23, 2026.

Before you go

  1. Book your flight to Jeju International Airport.
  2. Hiking to the top of Hallasan? Reserve at visithalla.jeju.go.kr as soon as bookings open, and pack ID that matches your booking.
  3. Bring a transit card such as T-money for the buses.
  4. Install Naver Map to plan bus routes.
  5. Check Seongsan Ilchulbong's closing day for your month (first Monday, or the next day after a Monday holiday).
  6. Save the emergency numbers: 119 and 112.

Source: Hallasan National Park, Korea Tourism Organization and Visit Jeju; checked Oct 23, 2026.

FAQ

Do I need a reservation to hike Hallasan?

To reach the summit, yes. The Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa trails run on reservations at visithalla.jeju.go.kr. You can only enter at your booked time, and you need ID that matches your booking.

What time do I have to start the Hallasan summit hike?

Entry opens at 05:00. Hikers heading for the summit must pass the trail entrance and the summit-route checkpoint (Jindallaebat or Samgakbong) by 11:30 from October to March, or 12:30 from April to September, so start well before that.

How much is Seongsan Ilchulbong?

₩5,000 for adults and ₩2,500 for teenagers and children for the summit trail. There's also a free coastal trail. It's closed on the first Monday of each month, or the next day if that Monday is a holiday.

How much is the bus in Jeju?

₩1,150 on regular buses with a transit card. Express buses start at ₩2,000 and go up to ₩3,000 depending on distance.

What food is Jeju famous for?

Heukdwaeji, Jeju black pork, usually grilled and dipped in meljeot anchovy sauce.
